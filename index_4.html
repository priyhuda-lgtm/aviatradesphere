Services

Built for the moment an aircraft is grounded.

From the first quotation to final delivery, our team manages sourcing, certification, and logistics end to end — so your aircraft returns to service faster.

Global Tarmac Operations
01

24/7 AOG Support

When an aircraft is grounded, every minute counts. Our dedicated AOG desk manages quotation, certificate evaluation, order processing, and logistics around the clock — backed by our own dispatch network and trusted logistics partners for global delivery.

02

Component Sales & Exchange

An extensive inventory of certified rotable spares, with exchange and loan programs that let airlines replace unserviceable parts without inflating stock or repair overhead.

03

Dangerous Goods Handling

Full IATA-compliant preparation and shipment of aircraft spares classified as dangerous goods, for air, road, and sea transport — even on short notice.

04

Buy & Sell Inventory

We acquire surplus and BER (beyond economical repair) inventory directly from airlines, arranging logistics at our expense and turning idle stock into revenue — with sizeable offers on complete lot submissions.

05

Repair Management

Close partnerships with EASA/FAA-approved repair shops covering over 48,000 part numbers, with guaranteed turnaround and exchange units available while your components are recertified.

06

Global Freight & Customs

Coordinated import/export documentation and freight management across our vendor and carrier network, keeping parts moving from warehouse to wheels-up.
